## Asset Resources

- [Flayo](https://flayodev.com/)

- [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/)  
  A popular source of free game development assets, including UI, 2D/3D art, audio, fonts, and game templates.

- [UI Resources](https://ui-resources.com/)
  A curated collection of free UI design resources, including UI kits, plugins, textures, effects, tutorials, and design tools.

- [Freesound](https://freesound.org/)  
  A large community-driven library of free sound effects, field recordings, and audio samples for game development and creative projects.

- [ZapSplat](https://www.zapsplat.com/)  
  A large library of sound effects and royalty-free music for games, videos, and creative projects.

- [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com/)  
  A free Adobe tool for auto-rigging 3D characters and applying ready-made character animations.
